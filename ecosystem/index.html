Field guide

Every creature carries inherited movement, sensing, metabolism, aggression, fertility, color, and controller weights. Food turns into energy; energy enables reproduction; small mutations make every generation a little different. Toxic vents punish careless foraging.

Paint into the tank to change the selection pressure. Cyan is food, blue-green is fertile habitat, and coral is toxic. Worlds are deterministic: the same seed and the same actions produce the same history.
